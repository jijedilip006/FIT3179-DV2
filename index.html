Australian Beaches: Water Quality & Safety Analysis

A visual exploration of NSW pollution metrics and Victorian beach hazards.

Part 1: New South Wales Water Quality

An overview of water quality ratings and pollution forecasts across NSW monitoring sites.

Water Quality Map

This proportional symbol map reveals the geographic spread of water quality monitoring sites across the New South Wales coast. The concentration of monitoring stations is heavily biased toward populous coastal hubs, providing critical safety data where beachgoer volume is highest.

Water Quality Ratings

The lollipop chart demonstrates the distribution of overall water quality ratings. It highlights the frequency of highly-rated beaches against those that struggle to meet safe recreational swimming standards.

Pollution Forecasts

Complementing the historical ratings, this donut chart breaks down short-term pollution forecasts, allowing us to see the exact proportion of coastal sites currently facing potential stormwater or bacterial contamination.

Part 2: Victorian Beach Safety & Facilities

Analyzing the relationship between physical hazards, natural features, and beach amenities across Victoria.

Victorian Beach Density Map

Using spatial binning, this density map illustrates the heavy concentration of beaches along the Victorian coastline. The darkest blue squares immediately draw attention to the densely populated clusters around Port Phillip Bay, contrasting sharply with the sparser, remote regional coastlines.

Distribution of Hazard Ratings

The hazard ratings for Victorian beaches form a distinct bell curve peaking around levels 6 and 7. This indicates that a significant majority of the state's beaches pose a moderate to high natural risk to swimmers, rather than being perfectly calm environments.

Hazards vs. BBQ Facilities

This heatmap uncovers a fascinating correlation between natural danger and human infrastructure. Beaches equipped with family-friendly amenities, such as BBQs, are overwhelmingly clustered in the lower, safer hazard categories (ratings 1-4).

Shark Presence & Hazard Ratings

Analyzing specific biological dangers, the box plot shows a clear upward shift in the median hazard rating for beaches where shark risks are documented. The presence of apex predators reliably correlates with a higher overall danger classification.

Physical Features vs. Hazard Ratings

This interactive scatter plot confirms a strict, positive correlation between a beach's inherent physical topography rating and its final hazard score. It suggests that natural structural elements, such as reef breaks and outfalls, are the primary dictators of swimming safety.
